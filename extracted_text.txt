PART 2 -- PRODUCTS  
2.1 SUBMERGED TURBINE AERATORS:  The units will provide aeration and mixing in each 
zone of aeration for a complete mix activated sludge treatment process. All aerators shall include 
the motor, drive mechanism, gearbox, support structure with vibration absorbers, impellers, shaft, 
bottom bearing guide, air spargers, OSHA required guards, and all other parts and fittings 
required to place the aerator in operation.  
 
The turbine and air sparger assembly shall be compatible with the concrete structures shown on 
the drawings. The assemblies shall be suitable for this oxygen transfer at an elevation of 5,100 
feet and a sludge temperature range of 40 to 100 degrees Fahrenheit. The new assemblies shall 
be adequate for creating a completely mixed flow regime in domestic wastewater. The dimensions

46 41 00 Submerged Turbine Mixers: 8/1/2025: 65420422   46 41 00-2  Submerged metal parts shall be 304 stainless steel; if welded, the metal shall be 304L. 
The air sparger will be compatible with the turbine for providing adequate oxygen transfer. The 
air sparger shall connect to air piping by 6-inch Class 125 standard flanged end connections. 
Provide supports suitable for anchoring the air sparger to the concrete floor. 
The aerator shall be designed to adequately mix and keep in suspension aerated solids within a 
zone of influence equal to the basin size shown on the Drawings. 
The associated motors shall be 480-volt, 3-phase motor with motor protection (type IP 55 or IP 
65), and thermal protection using PTC resistors. The motor HP shall not be greater than 12.5- or 
20-horsepower (altitude corrected), as indicated on the Drawings. The motor will be driven using 
a constant speed motor.  
AGMA gear calculations and bearing rating calculations are to be submitted and shall be equal to 
an AGMA Class I for the motor horsepower at design output speed and 80,000 hours of B-10 life 
at this rating. 
The shaft bearing assembly shall be designed to operate at or below 0.7 of the natural frequency 
of the system.  
The aerator turbine shaft shall be stainless-steel with flanged connection to the gearbox shaft and 
impeller. 
Each agitator shall be furnished with a turbine-type impeller. The maximum impeller diameter 
shall be 96-inches. The mixer body will be composed of high-quality fiberglass reinforced plastic 
(FRP) or stainless steel. The bottom bearing guide shall be made from stainless fixed below the 
unit on the basin floor.  
The Contractor shall furnish HyperClassic Mixer/Aerator units by INVENT or AER-GS mixer units 
by AquaTurbo.
